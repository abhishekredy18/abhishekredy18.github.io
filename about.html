About Me

I am a Junior AI/ML Engineer at SPAN Enterprises, where I build production AI systems — from a call analytics platform powered by Whisper and self-hosted LLMs to LangChain/LangGraph chatbots with tool calling and MCP integrations.

I completed my Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU), Pittsburgh, with a focus on Materials Science and Engineering, and graduated in December 2025 with a 3.82 GPA.

Prior to CMU, I completed my Bachelor of Technology in Mechanical Engineering at the National Institute of Technology Calicut, India.

My graduate coursework included Computer Vision, Systems and Tool Chains for AI Engineers, Trustworthy AI, and Deep Learning & LLMs. I am passionate about applying AI to solve complex engineering challenges.
