About Me

I am a Junior AI/ML Engineer at SPAN Enterprises, where I build production AI systems — from a call analytics platform powered by Whisper and self-hosted LLMs to LangChain/LangGraph chatbots with tool calling and MCP integrations.

My coursework includes Computer Vision, Systems and Tool Chains for AI Engineers, Trustworthy AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and more. I am passionate about applying AI to solve complex engineering challenges.
