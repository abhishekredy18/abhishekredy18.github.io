## Problem

Mining autonomous-driving logs for specific scenarios — "a pedestrian
walking between parked vehicles" — normally requires hand-built filters per
scenario. Free-form language would make scenario mining accessible and
fast, but standard 3D trackers don't understand text.

## Approach

I built a language-grounded 3D tracking model that maps free-form text
prompts to 3D bounding boxes in driving scenes, enabling text-based
scenario mining over the Argoverse 2 dataset.

## Architecture & Tech

The model fuses text, LiDAR point clouds, HD map data, and vehicle pose in
a transformer architecture inspired by PromptTrack, aligning language
queries with multimodal sensor data. Argoverse 2 provides rich real-world
urban driving data for training and evaluation.

## Results

The system demonstrates text-prompt-driven 3D object detection and
tracking across video sequences, supporting automated identification of
safety-critical scenarios for autonomous-vehicle testing and validation.
