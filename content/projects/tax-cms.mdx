## Problem

TaxBandits.com and Tax990.com each need accurate product content in two
places at once: the public product pages and the AI assistant that answers
customer questions. Maintaining them separately meant duplicated work and
the risk of the chatbot answering from stale knowledge.

## Approach

I created a central content management system in Python and React that
powers both — product content and chatbot knowledge ship from a single
place, so an edit made once reaches the pages and the assistant together.

## Architecture & Tech

Role-based access control governs users, permissions, and content
workflows. CMS operations are also exposed as Model Context Protocol (MCP)
tools, so AI agents can read and manage content through the same governed
interface humans use.

The customer-facing chatbot and its feedback loop are built on LangChain
and LangGraph, with agentic tool calling wired to the TaxBandits developer
APIs — the assistant looks up real product capabilities instead of
guessing. Models fine-tuned and deployed on RunPod extract structured data
from user-uploaded tax form images, feeding validated fields straight into
the product workflows.

## Results

One governed system now feeds the live TaxBandits.com and Tax990.com
applications and their AI assistant, with agent-accessible CMS operations
and automated tax-form extraction in production.
