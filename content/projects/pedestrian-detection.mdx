## Problem

Urban intersections need immediate, local safety intervention: alerts when
pedestrians enter the road during restricted signals, or when vehicles
approach at unsafe speeds. Cloud inference adds latency and cost — the
detection has to run in real time on cheap edge hardware.

## Approach

I designed and deployed a real-time pedestrian detection and safety alert
system on the NVIDIA Jetson Nano, comparing YOLO and MobileNet SSD
architectures trained on the CityPersons and Caltech pedestrian datasets.

## Architecture & Tech

To reach real-time performance on resource-constrained hardware, the models
were quantized to INT8, pruned of redundant parameters, and accelerated
with TensorRT. The DeepStream SDK handles efficient video processing with
frame batching and multi-stream support.

## Results

The system triggers audio-visual alerts when pedestrians enter the road
during restricted signals and flashes warnings for vehicles approaching at
unsafe speeds during red lights, with low-latency detection running
entirely on the edge device.
