## Problem

A 50-person customer support team handles more than 9,000 calls per month.
Team leads had no practical way to review call quality: listening to
recordings doesn't scale, and sensitive customer conversations couldn't be
sent to a third-party analytics service.

## Approach

I built a production call analytics platform that turns raw recordings into
searchable transcripts, concise summaries, and per-representative
performance metrics — giving leads a continuously updated view of call
quality without listening to a single recording, and without any call data
leaving the company.

## Architecture & Tech

Whisper converts every recording into accurate, timestamped transcripts,
and speaker diarization separates representative and customer speech so
metrics and summaries attribute statements to the right speaker. A
self-hosted Qwen 8B LLM scores sentiment, classifies call topics, and flags
risky calls for review.

On top of the analyzed corpus sits a grounded chat assistant that answers
natural-language questions and cites the source calls behind every answer,
keeping responses anchored to what was actually said rather than model
speculation.

The entire platform is deployed with Docker on the company's internal
network — transcription, LLM inference, and search all run in-house.

## Results

The support organization tracks call quality across 9,000+ calls per month
through searchable transcripts and per-representative metrics, and leads
query the corpus conversationally through the citation-backed assistant.
