IDD-AW addresses the need for safety and robustness in semantic segmentation for autonomous driving under adverse weather (rain, fog, low light, snow) and unstructured traffic. It provides 5,000 high-quality paired RGB and Near-Infrared (NIR) images with dense pixel-level annotations across a hierarchical label set, captured across diverse Indian road conditions. To better quantify dangerous mispredictions, the work introduces Safe mean Intersection over Union (Safe mIoU), a hierarchy-aware metric that penalizes unsafe classification errors more heavily than traditional mIoU. The paper benchmarks state-of-the-art models on IDD-AW, demonstrating the challenges posed by adverse weather and the benefits of the new dataset and metric.
